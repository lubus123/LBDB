    Getting around

    • IC card. Non-Japanese Android phones cannot run Mobile Suica. Buy two physical Welcome Suica cards at the JR East Travel Service Center in Narita T2 (28-day validity, no deposit). Works on every metro, JR local line, bus, and konbini nationwide.
    • Narita → hotel. Narita Express to Tokyo Station, ¥3,070, 60 min, then taxi or Marunouchi line to Akasaka. Evening arrival, so the train beats the limousine bus.
    • Kyoto. Buses are slow in November; use the Karasuma and Tozai subway lines, JR, and Keihan where possible. Fushimi Inari is two JR stops from Kyoto Station.
    • Luggage. Optional: ship the big bag Tokyo → Kyoto by takkyubin from the hotel (about ¥2,500 per bag, next day) and travel light on the shinkansen.
    • Connectivity. eSIM (Ubigi or Airalo) bought before departure; offline Google Maps for Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, Hiroshima, Hakone.
    Intel

    Rules of engagement

    • Sat 31 Oct, Halloween. Shibuya station area is barricaded, street drinking banned from 18:00. Shibuya is on 29 Oct instead; Shinjuku on the 31st.
    • Tue 3 Nov, Culture Day. National holiday. Fushimi Inari at 07:00 before the crowds, Arashiyama after, Kinkaku-ji late afternoon.
    • Ryokan timing. Check in by 17:00, kaiseki dinner is served around 18:00 and cannot be moved. Leave Shin-Osaka by 12:00 on 8 Nov.
    • Onsen etiquette. Wash before entering, no swimwear, hair up, small towel stays out of the water. Tattoos: ask at check-in or book a private bath.
    • Hiroshima. Miyajima first, before 09:30, to beat the tour groups on the ferry. Peace Memorial Museum is open to 18:00, last entry 17:30, ¥200, no reservation. Miyajima charges a ¥100 visitor tax.
    • Haneda exfil. Flight 09:35 means at Terminal 3 by 07:30. From Tenkubashi it is one Keikyu stop; from the Kamata hotels it is 10 min.
    • Cash. Cards work in cities, but shrines, small restaurants, and Miyajima stalls want yen. Withdraw at 7-Eleven ATMs.
    • Visa paperwork. The visa itinerary was filed for 31 Oct to 13 Nov. The flights land two days earlier. A shift inside the visa's validity window is fine if it is already issued; if still pending, resubmit with the new dates.
